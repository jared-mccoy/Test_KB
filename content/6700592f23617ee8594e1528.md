We drift across the surface like specks of dust stuck to a gigantic eye -- a glossy rotundity on which truth and opinion hang suspended across the horizon like the skin of a drum. Wherever we find ourselves is always already traversed from every direction. The history of these crossings hangs heavy in the awful stillness of the moment.[^1]

[^1]: Cf. Derrida's treatment of Husserl's [[67216bfb7ac68fa3c01999ff|ἐποχή (epoché)]] e.g. his critique of  [[67216bfbb41aa7693e61ce85|Ideas]] in [[67216bfb5aafb62e55cef87f|Voice and Phenomenon]] and countless occasions thereafter. See also Stiegler's numerous formulations of the epoché in [[66fc84b5efc8f62f56f36681|Technics and Time]] and other works. Cf. [[67216bfbbe8ff48db87c34e2|Moby Dick]] chapter 39 "The Castaway" for a particularly striking depiction of what we might call the [[67216bfbe0c33780dd60fce9|vertigo]] of the surface. Cf. Heidegger's concept of 'Befindlichkeit' ([[66bfc258620c1ac13d665991|Being and Time (Division One )]]) as well as Nietzsche's "[[67216bfb0750dd4ccab6db15|On the Vision and the Riddle]]" ([[67216bfb3e1a7db0f2a868cb|Thus Spake Zarathustra]])
