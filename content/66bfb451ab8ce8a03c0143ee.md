Fascinating to think of this property of chemical / molecular structure as a figure for the relationship between [[66bfc25896395492fa9a513e|Vorhandenheit / Zuhandenheit]] in [[66bfc258620c1ac13d665991|division one of Being and Time]] . Or, more generally, in terms of [[66bfc258442b43a0d56df43e|ontic-ontological difference]] and its many reformulations throughout Heidegger's late writings. I've always seen Vorhandenheit / Zuhandenheit as an exploration of the latter from a distinctly spatial perspective (although I can't recall at the moment what the exact temporal [[66bfc258265c5d5776151174|(division two)]] analogs for  these would be ... but, probably something that plays on the presence of the present as a moment versus the coming-to-presence that he explores through the [[66bfc2585a6fdb3bb84989fa|Wesen / Anwesen / Abwesen]] cluster. 

This idea of being symmetrical but not superimposable has all sorts of affinities with the [[666b6fad34edecf4c27ef9a4|Derrida]], [[666b763aa932badb1cddf57a|de Man]], et al, as well. Could easily be used to express the problem of [[66bfc2589d37f1eb529ffa63|irreducibility]] in general. 

The fittingness of the figure of the hand for Heidegger in particular though, seems the most interesting. After all, around these critical epicenters of ex-centricity and   critique one can always analogize anything with with anything else. The idea of chirality for Heidegger though, fits hand in glove.[^1]

Especially in the way that the duality of the ontic-ontological perspectives, through the lens of the later seminars at least, appears to be nothing of  the sort. That is, distinctly *not* a difference of to divisible vantage points on experience and certainly not a contrast between some phenomenal pseudotruth and an elevated philosophical 'understanding'. But rather an originary event ([[666b7a4172d325504959d4de|Ereignis]]) that breaks and binds them into an historical *being*.[^2]
